---
title: Dashboard Not Loading
description: Fix issues with GTM Fabric dashboard not displaying.
---

## Symptoms

- Blank or frozen dashboard page
- Error: "Unable to load data"

## Solutions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear Your Cache">
    ````
    Refresh browser or clear site cache in settings.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Network">
    ````
    Ensure you have internet access and firewall allows `dashboard.gtmfabric.com`.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try Incognito Mode">
    ````
    Open dashboard in private/incognito browser window.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
