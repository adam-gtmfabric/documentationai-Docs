## Quick Start

1. Go to `https://dashboard.gtmfabric.com`.
2. Sign up using your business email.
3. Verify your email address.

## Next Steps

- Complete your company profile
- Invite your team members
- Explore the "Partners" tab to discover integrations

For detailed onboarding, see our [Guides](/help-center/guides/invite-a-partner).
