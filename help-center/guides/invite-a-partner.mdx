---
title: Inviting a Partner to GTM Fabric
description: Quickly invite a new partner organization to collaborate.
---

## Overview

Invite partners to streamline ecosystem collaboration.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Partners Tab">
    ````
    Navigate to the "Partners" tab in your GTM Fabric dashboard.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invite">
    ````
    Click "Invite Partner", enter the partner's email, and select permissions.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Partner Accepts">
    ````
    Your partner will receive a link to join and set up their company profile.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
